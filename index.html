What are Floods?

A flood is a natural disaster that occurs when water overflows onto land that is normally dry. Floods usually happen due to heavy rainfall, overflowing rivers, melting snow, dam failure, or coastal storms.

What Are Floods

Floods occur when water levels rise beyond the capacity of rivers, lakes, or drainage systems, causing water to spread over land. They can develop slowly over days or suddenly within hours, depending on weather conditions and geography.

Flash Floods

Flash floods are extremely rapid floods caused by intense rainfall, cloudbursts, or sudden release of water from dams. They develop within minutes or hours and are highly dangerous because they provide little or no warning.

Causes of Floods

Common causes include prolonged heavy rain, blocked drainage systems, river overflow, deforestation, urbanization, and cyclones. In hilly regions, flash floods are often triggered by cloudbursts.

Effects of Floods

Floods can lead to loss of life, destruction of property, spread of waterborne diseases, displacement of people, and long-term economic damage.

Precautions During Flood

When floods are expected, follow these essential precautions to ensure safety.

Move to Higher Grounds

If water levels are rising, move to the roof or an upper floor and alert neighbors.

Keep Emergency Kits Ready

Include water, non-perishable food, flashlight, batteries, medicines, and important documents.

Avoid Walking/Driving Through Floodwater

Just 15 cm of fast-moving water can knock a person down; 30 cm can move a car.

Unplug Electrical Devices

Turn off the main power supply before water reaches electrical outlets. Avoid contact with electrical equipment if wet.

Flood Resistant House

Flood-resistant houses combine modern architecture with safety engineering. Built to float or stand above flood levels, these designs focus on sustainability, safety, and affordability.

  • Elevated on sturdy and reinforced platforms
  • Use of waterproof materials and hydrophobic coatings
  • Solar panels and rainwater harvesting systems
  • Green surroundings that improve soil drainage
